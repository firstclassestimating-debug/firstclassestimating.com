What we look for

Built from your file, item by item.

These are the items that commonly get left off or underpaid on carrier estimates. We go through every one against your photos, measurements and local code.

We never add damage your file doesn't support. Good photos and a complete checklist make a stronger supplement, and our checklist makes that easy for your crew.
  • Starter strip at eaves and rakes, drip edge, gutter apron
  • Ice and water shield in valleys
  • Steep and high-roof charges, additional layers, felt
  • Pipe jacks, vents, step, counter and chimney flashing
  • Waste and measurement differences against the carrier's sketch
  • Gutters, screens, garage doors, fences and other exterior damage
  • Solar detach & reset, satellites and roof-mounted items
  • Texas code items, permits, and overhead & profit where it applies

FAQ

Questions roofers ask us

Want the full rundown before we talk? Read our How We Work letter.

Are you a public adjuster?

No. We're your estimating and supplement team. We document the scope and submit it for you, the contractor. We don't represent or advise the homeowner.

Do you ever talk to my homeowners?

Never. Your customers stay yours. We work only with you and the carrier.

Do you use Xactimate?

Yes. Estimates are written line by line in the Xactimate format carriers use, priced on the current Texas price list.

How fast do you turn a claim around?

We submit within 48 hours of a complete file. How fast the carrier decides is up to the carrier, which is why we stay on the adjuster until there's an answer.

What if the supplement gets denied?

You don't owe us anything for that claim.

What about a partial approval?

We keep pushing for the items that weren't approved and invoice once the supplement decision is final.

Do you handle the COC or depreciation?

No. Our work ends when the carrier decides on the supplement. COC, proof of deductible and recoverable depreciation stay with you.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. It's a short agreement with no term. Either of us can end it anytime by email.

Can I send several claims at once?

Yes. One request per claim, and each one gets worked on its own.

What areas and carriers?

Texas claims, focused on DFW. Any carrier.
