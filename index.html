Questions roofers ask us

Want the full rundown first? Read our How We Work letter.

Are you a public adjuster?

No. We're your estimating and supplement team. We document the scope and submit it for you, the contractor. We don't represent or advise the homeowner.

Do you ever talk to my homeowners?

Never. Your customers stay yours. We work only with you and the carrier.

Do you use Xactimate?

Yes. Estimates are written line by line in the Xactimate format carriers use, priced on the current Texas price list.

How fast do you turn a claim around?

We submit within 48 hours of a complete file. How fast the carrier decides is up to the carrier, which is why we stay on the adjuster until there's an answer.

Is the first estimate really free?

Yes. Your first supplement estimate costs nothing, start to finish, whatever the carrier approves. We just ask to use it as a case study with the homeowner's details removed.

What if the supplement gets denied?

You don't owe us anything for that claim.

What about a partial approval?

We keep pushing for the items that weren't approved and invoice once the carrier's decision is final.

How do I pay?

By Zelle. Every invoice has the details and is due 14 days after the approval.

Do you handle the COC or depreciation?

No. Our work ends when the carrier decides on the supplement. The certificate of completion, proof of deductible and recoverable depreciation stay with you.

Can I send several claims at once?

Yes. One request per claim, and each one gets worked on its own.

What areas and carriers?

Texas claims, focused on Dallas-Fort Worth. Any carrier.
